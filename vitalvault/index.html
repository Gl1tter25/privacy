VitalVault Privacy Policy

VitalVault is a local-first log for manually tracked blood glucose and blood pressure. Your readings live in an encrypted on-device vault, AI insight cards run entirely on your phone, and the only network surface is a single banner ad, purchases, and the on-device model download. This policy explains exactly that.

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the VitalVault Android application (the “App”), published by Glitter, based in Hong Kong (“Glitter,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains how information is accessed, used, stored, disclosed, retained, and deleted when you use the App. The App's package name is com.glitterfan.vitalvault.

VitalVault is a personal record-keeping and trend-reporting tool for self-measured blood glucose and blood pressure. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Reports and AI insight cards are observations about your own entries, not medical advice. Always consult a qualified clinician.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • blood glucose readings (value, timing context such as fasting or after meal, notes, time);
  • blood pressure readings (systolic, diastolic, pulse, context, notes, time);
  • your preferred units and custom reference ranges;
  • reminder, display, app-lock, privacy, and export settings; and
  • information contained in exports or backups that you create.

This information is health-related and sensitive. It is used on your device to provide logging, history, statistics, trend charts, PDF reports, reminders, and the optional AI insight cards. Glitter does not operate a server that receives any of these user-entered records.

3. Local processing and storage

VitalVault stores user-entered records and settings in an encrypted database in the App's private storage on your Android device (SQLCipher; the database key is wrapped by Android Keystore). Android's system cloud backup is disabled for the App's private data, so your records are not copied to Google Drive by the system.

The App does not require your name, phone number, email address, location, or an account, and it includes no third-party analytics SDK.

The optional “AI insights” feature runs entirely on your device using an on-device model (LiteRT-LM, delivered once as an install-time asset pack). Inference uses no internet connection, and your readings, notes, and statistics are never uploaded for AI processing. On devices that cannot meet the on-device model's memory requirement, the feature stays closed and the App continues with deterministic statistics only.

Important: readings, notes, reports, and AI outputs are never sent to Google AdMob. The single advertising request path is separate from the local record database and carries no health context and no custom targeting keys.

4. Advertising and automatically handled information

The free tier of the App shows one banner ad, on the Report tab only, provided by Google AdMob with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or share:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad impressions; and
  • diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance, launch time, hangs, and energy usage.

Google describes these uses as supporting advertising, measurement or analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Ad loading only starts after the consent check allows it.

Where required, the App presents a consent form before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit your choices through Mine → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy, Advertising Technologies, and Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

An optional, one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited on-device AI insight generations. It is sold through Google Play Billing; the purchase is processed entirely by Google and we never see or store your payment details — we only receive a purchase token used to unlock the feature on your device. Your readings are not involved in the purchase and are not sent to us as part of it.

The Pro purchase unlocks AI capacity only; it does not change the single banner ad described in Section 4, and every feature that was free stays free.

5. Sharing and sale of information

Glitter does not sell your readings, notes, or statistics, and does not share them with advertising services — we never receive them in the first place.

Information leaves the App only when you deliberately share a PDF report, export a vault file or plain files, and select another app, service, folder, or storage provider as the destination. That destination's privacy terms then apply. Google may process advertising-related information as described in Section 4 and billing information under Google's own policies.

6. Android permissions and device features

  • Internet and network state: used to request the banner ad and privacy messages, to verify purchases, and to deliver the install-time on-device model pack. Not used to upload your readings.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for local measurement reminders you configure yourself.
  • Biometric authentication (optional): used to lock the App when you enable App lock. Authentication is performed by Android; the App does not receive or store your fingerprint, face, or biometric template.

The App requests no other special permissions. Declining notifications disables only reminders; logging, statistics, PDF reports, and AI insights are unaffected.

7. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the App's local-first design: an encrypted on-device vault (SQLCipher) whose key is protected by Android Keystore, optional biometric app lock, disabled Android system backup for App-private data, encrypted-by-default vault exports, and the encrypted network transport used by Google advertising and billing services. AI inference runs locally and does not traverse the network.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any exported files, PDFs, or backups after they leave the App.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Active records remain on your device until you delete them individually.
  • The Settings “wipe all data” control permanently removes all records and settings from the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its private local storage under normal Android behavior.
  • Exports, backups, and PDF reports you created must be deleted separately from the folder, app, or storage provider where you saved them.

Glitter does not hold a server-side copy of your records and therefore cannot retrieve or delete those local records for you. Retention of advertising- and billing-related information handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and controls.

9. Age and children's privacy

VitalVault is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect user-entered personal information from children on our servers because the App has no developer-operated collection server.

If the law where you live requires a higher age to consent to data processing, use the App only with the permission and supervision required by your parent or legal guardian. If you believe advertising-related information has been handled in connection with a child contrary to applicable law, contact us and use Google's privacy controls.

10. International processing

Glitter is based in Hong Kong. User-entered records remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Google and its advertising and billing partners may process advertising- and purchase-related information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable transfer safeguards.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for certain processing.

  • Use the App's edit and delete controls, the Settings data wipe, or uninstall, for local records; use exports for portability.
  • Use Privacy options in the App where displayed, or Android's advertising privacy controls.
  • Contact Google regarding information controlled by Google.
  • Contact Glitter using the email address below for questions or requests concerning this policy.

Because Glitter does not receive your local records, we may be unable to identify or provide records that exist only on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, communicated in the App or its store listing.
