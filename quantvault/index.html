QuantVault Privacy Policy

QuantVault is a local-first quantified-self journal. Your trackers, entries and statistics never leave your phone — and its optional AI insights run entirely on-device too. This policy explains that local processing, the limited information handled by Google AdMob for advertising, and the optional paid AI features.

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the QuantVault Android application (the “App”), published by Glitter, based in Hong Kong (“Glitter,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains how information is accessed, used, stored, disclosed, retained, and deleted when you use the App. The App's package name is com.glitterfan.quantvault.

QuantVault is a personal record-keeping and statistics tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Insights the App produces are statistical observations about your own data, not professional advice.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • custom trackers and metrics (numeric scales, counters, durations, yes/no items, mood and energy scores);
  • dated entries, notes, tags, and daily events attached to those trackers;
  • reminder, display, language, privacy, and export settings; and
  • information contained in JSON exports that you create.

This information may be personal or otherwise sensitive. It is used on your device to provide recording, history, statistics, weekly summaries, and exports. Glitter does not operate a server that receives any of these user-entered records.

3. Local processing and storage

QuantVault stores user-entered records and settings in the App's private storage on your Android device. Android's cloud auto-backup is disabled for the App's private data, so your records are not copied to Google Drive by the system.

The App does not require your name, phone number, email address, location, or an account, and it includes no third-party analytics SDK.

The optional “insights” feature runs entirely on your device using an on-device model (Google ML Kit GenAI / LiteRT-LM). Inference uses no internet connection, and your entries, notes, and statistics are never uploaded for AI processing.

Important: Journal records, notes, and AI outputs are not sent to Google AdMob. Advertising requests are separate from the local journal database.

4. Advertising and automatically handled information

The free tier of the App uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to provide low-frequency ads and, where required, advertising privacy choices. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or share:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad impressions; and
  • diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance, launch time, hangs, and energy usage.

Google describes these uses as supporting advertising, measurement or analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Two deliberate product choices limit advertising further: no ads appear during the first 7 days after installation, and ads are restricted to secondary screens — the App's core recording screens contain no ad containers at all.

Where required, the App presents a consent form before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit your choices through Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy, Advertising Technologies, and Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

Optional paid AI features (such as the weekly narrative report) are sold through Google Play Billing. The purchase is processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment details. We only receive a purchase token that unlocks the feature on your device. Your journal data is not involved in the purchase and is not sent to us as part of it.

5. Sharing and sale of information

Glitter does not sell your journal records, notes, or statistics, and does not share them with advertising services — we never receive them in the first place.

Information leaves the App only when you deliberately export a JSON file and select another app, service, folder, or storage provider as the destination. That destination's privacy terms then apply. Google may process advertising-related information as described in Section 4 and under Google's own policies.

6. Android permissions and device features

  • Internet and network state: used to request ads and privacy messages, and to download the optional on-device AI model pack. Not used to upload your journal data.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for local reminder notifications you configure yourself.

The App requests no other special permissions. Declining notifications disables only reminders; record keeping, statistics, and AI insights are unaffected.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the App's local-first design, including Android private app storage, disabled Android system backup for App-private data, and the encrypted network transport used by Google advertising services. The optional model pack is downloaded over HTTPS and verified before use.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any exported files after they leave the App.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Active records remain on your device until you delete them.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its private local storage under normal Android behavior.
  • JSON exports you created must be deleted separately from the folder, app, or storage provider where you saved them.

Glitter does not hold a server-side copy of your journal records and therefore cannot retrieve or delete those local records for you. Retention of advertising-related information handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and controls.

9. Age and children's privacy

QuantVault is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect user-entered personal information from children on our servers because the App has no developer-operated collection server.

10. International processing

Glitter is based in Hong Kong. User-entered journal records remain on your device unless you choose to export them. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable transfer safeguards.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for certain processing.

  • Use the App's edit and delete controls, or uninstall, for local records; use JSON export for portability.
  • Use Privacy options in the App where displayed, or Android's advertising privacy controls.
  • Contact Google regarding information controlled by Google.
  • Contact Glitter using the email address below for questions or requests concerning this policy.

Because Glitter does not receive your journal records, we may be unable to identify or provide records that exist only on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, communicated in the App or its store listing.
