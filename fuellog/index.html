FuelLog Privacy Policy

FuelLog is a private fuel log: every fill record and setting lives only on your device. This policy explains that local processing and the limited information handled by Google AdMob for advertising.

Effective 5 September 2026 Last updated 5 September 2026 For users aged 18+ Authors: Glitter & Ezra
On-device onlyFuel entries and settings are stored in the app's local database and are never sent to Glitter or to advertising services.
No account requiredFuelLog has no account system, no cloud sync, and no separate analytics SDK.
Your choicesYou can add, edit or delete records, clear all app data via uninstall, and manage advertising privacy choices at any time.

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the FuelLog Android application (the “App”), published by Glitter, based in Hong Kong (“Glitter,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains how information is accessed, used, stored, disclosed, retained, and deleted when you use the App. The App's package name is com.glitterfan.fuellog.

Disclaimer: FuelLog only keeps your own fuel records on this device. It is not a gas station, not a price service, and does not give driving advice. It does not connect to any fuel station, fuel-price database, or driving-coach service.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • the date of each fill;
  • your odometer reading (in kilometres);
  • how many liters you added, and whether you filled the tank;
  • the optional total cost of the fill; and
  • display and privacy settings.

This information is used on your device to keep your fuel ledger and its economy overview. Glitter does not operate a server that receives these user-entered records.

3. Local processing and storage

FuelLog stores user-entered records and settings in the App's private storage on your Android device (an on-device Room database). Android system cloud backup is disabled for the App, and app-private data is excluded from device data-transfer tools.

The App does not require your name, phone number, email address, precise location, or an account. It does not include cloud synchronization or a separate analytics SDK.

Important: Your fuel records, odometer, liters, and costs are not sent to Google AdMob. Advertising requests are separate from the local record database.

4. Advertising and automatically handled information

The App shows a single banner ad (on the Log screen) using Google AdMob, and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices where required. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or share:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad impressions; and
  • diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance, launch time, hangs, and energy usage.

Google describes these uses as supporting advertising, measurement or analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Where required, the App presents a consent form before requesting ads; where available, you can revisit your choices through Settings → Privacy options.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy, Advertising Technologies, and Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

5. Sharing and sale of information

Glitter does not sell your user-entered fuel records, settings, or statistics. We do not share those records with advertising services.

Your information leaves the App only if you deliberately share content of your own (for example by screenshot or another app you choose). Google may process advertising-related information as described in Section 4 and under Google's own policies.

6. Android permissions and device features

  • Internet and network state: used only to request ads and privacy messages (the advertising SDK adds these automatically).
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising SDK as described in Section 2; you can reset or delete it in Android settings.

The App requests no notification, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or biometric permissions.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the App's local-only design: Android private app storage, disabled Android system backup and data-extraction for app data, and encrypted network transport (TLS) used by Google advertising services.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Records remain on your device until you delete them.
  • You can delete individual entries inside the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its private local storage under normal Android behavior.

Glitter does not hold a server-side copy of your records and therefore cannot retrieve or delete those local records for you. Retention of advertising-related information handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and controls.

9. Age and children's privacy

FuelLog is intended for users 18+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect user-entered information on our servers (there is none). If you believe advertising-related information has been handled in connection with a minor contrary to applicable law, contact us and use Google's privacy controls.

10. International processing

Glitter is based in Hong Kong. User-entered records remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable transfer safeguards.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or withdraw consent for processing.

  • Use the App's controls to manage or delete local records, or uninstall the App.
  • Use Privacy options in the App's settings where displayed, or Android's advertising controls.
  • Contact Glitter or Google using the details below.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, communicated in the App or its store listing.
