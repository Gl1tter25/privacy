WrenchVault Privacy Policy

WrenchVault is a private maintenance log for your own vehicles: every service record, mileage figure, cost and note lives only on your device. This policy explains that local processing and the limited information handled by Google AdMob for advertising.

Effective 4 September 2026 Last updated 4 September 2026 For users aged 18+ Authors: Glitter & Ezra

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the WrenchVault Android application (the “App”), published by Glitter, based in Hong Kong (“Glitter,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). It explains how information is accessed, used, stored, disclosed, retained, and deleted when you use the App. The App's package name is com.glitterfan.wrenchvault.

WrenchVault only keeps the maintenance records you enter, on this device. It is not a repair shop and gives no mechanical advice.

2. Information you provide

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • a name for each vehicle you track;
  • service-typed records (oil, tires, brakes, inspection, other) with a date;
  • an optional odometer reading (km) and an optional cost; and
  • optional notes for each service, plus display, language, and privacy settings.

This information is used on your device to keep your maintenance history and its overviews. Glitter does not operate a server that receives these user-entered records.

3. Local processing and storage

WrenchVault stores user-entered records and settings in the App's private storage on your Android device (an on-device Room database). Android system cloud backup is disabled for the App, and app-private data is excluded from device data-transfer tools.

The App does not require your name, phone number, email address, precise location, or an account. It does not include cloud synchronization or a separate analytics SDK.

Important: Service dates, odometer readings, costs, and notes are not sent to Google AdMob. Advertising requests are separate from the local record database.

4. Advertising and automatically handled information

The App shows a single banner ad (on the History screen) using Google AdMob, and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices where required. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or share:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad impressions; and
  • diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance.

Google describes these uses as supporting advertising, measurement or analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Where required, the App presents a consent form before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit your choices through Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. New installs are ad-free for the first 7 days, and the banner appears only on the History screen.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Advertising Technologies, and Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

5. Sharing and sale of information

Glitter does not sell your user-entered records, settings, or statistics, and does not share those records with advertising services. Your information leaves the App only if you deliberately share content of your own (for example by screenshot or another app you choose). Google may process advertising-related information as described in Section 4 under Google's own policies.

6. Android permissions and device features

  • Internet and network state: used only to request ads and privacy messages (the advertising SDK adds these permissions automatically).
  • Advertising identifier: used by the advertising SDK as described in section 4.

The App requests no notification, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or biometric permissions.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the App's local-only design: Android private app storage, disabled Android system backup and data-extraction for app data, and encrypted network transport (TLS) used by Google advertising services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Records remain on your device until you delete them.
  • You can delete individual services inside the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its private local storage under normal Android behavior.

Glitter does not hold a server-side copy of your records and therefore cannot retrieve or delete those local records for you. Advertising-related information handled by Google is governed by Google's policies and controls.

9. Age and children's privacy

WrenchVault is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. The App has no developer-operated collection server.

10. International processing

Glitter is based in Hong Kong. User-entered records remain on your device unless you choose to share them. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable transfer safeguards.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Use the App's service controls for local records, or uninstall the App.
  • Use privacy choices in the App's settings where displayed, or Android's advertising privacy controls.
  • Contact Glitter using the email address below for questions about this policy.

Because Glitter does not receive your local records, we may be unable to provide records that exist only on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, communicated in the App or its store listing.
